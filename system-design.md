## Suggested path

1. **Tier 0 → Tier 1 → Tier 2**, in that order. This is what freshers are actually tested on.
2. **Tier 3** case studies: do one a week, designing it yourself on paper before reading the solution.
3. **Tier 6 build-it-yourself** projects alongside the above. They give you real depth for infra roles.
4. **Tier 4** only when applying to FAANG-style loops. **Tier 5** whenever you want an edge, and read the DDIA chapters it points to.
5. **Tier 7**: one outage post-mortem a week as light background reading.
